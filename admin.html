Finance & Membership

Private administration stays in Google Sheets

This GitHub Pages site is intentionally public-only. Do not put member phone numbers, dues balances, payment references, or a password-protected “admin database” in the repository.

1. Members

Maintain names, town, chiefdom, WhatsApp number, member ID, dues start month, and secure access token in the private Members sheet.

2. Payments

Review submitted payment confirmations. Change Status from PENDING to VERIFIED only after confirming receipt.

3. WhatsApp Queue

Use the custom KAIDA menu in Google Sheets to rebuild personalized reminders and secure portal links.

Fee rules

Registration fee: $50
Monthly dues: $10

The backend calculates expected dues by month. These values can be changed in the Settings sheet without changing the website code.

Never commit the private member CSV or Google Sheet export to GitHub.
